Privacy

What classmates and admins can see about you.

  • Your name and photo are visible to classmates in your own class only, plus your school's admin.
  • Uploads you post are visible to classmates in the same school and class — not to other schools or classes.
  • Your email address is never shown to classmates, only to admins for account support.
  • Your XP, streak, and rank are visible to classmates on the class Leaderboard.

Want your account or data removed entirely? Contact your school's admin — they can do this from the admin panel.
