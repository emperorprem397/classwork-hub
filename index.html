ABOUT

Built for the day you're not there.

Classwork Hub started as a simple idea — if you're absent, you shouldn't have to hunt through five different WhatsApp groups to find out what you missed. Classmates upload photos of the day's notebook work, tag what it is, and it's there for anyone who needs it.
